Help & User Guide

Step-by-step instructions for using the clinic management system.

Admin User Administrator

Welcome to Sunrise Dental Clinic

This guide provides simple instructions for staff members to perform the main tasks in the Sunrise Dental Clinic Management System.

1

Login to the System

  1. Open the Sunrise Dental Clinic system.
  2. Enter your authorized username.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click the Login button.
  5. The dashboard will open after successful authentication.
2

Register a New Patient

  1. Open the Patients section.
  2. Enter the patient's name.
  3. Enter the patient's address.
  4. Enter the contact number.
  5. Click Register Patient.
  6. The patient will be saved in the system.
3

Register an Appointment

  1. Open the Appointments section.
  2. Enter the appointment number.
  3. Enter the patient's information.
  4. Select the dentist.
  5. Select the required treatment.
  6. Select the appointment date and time.
  7. Click Register Appointment.
4

Find Appointment Details

  1. Open the appointment search section.
  2. Enter the appointment number.
  3. Click Search.
  4. The patient's appointment information will be displayed.
5

Calculate & Print Bill

  1. Open the Billing section.
  2. Select or enter the treatment.
  3. Enter the consultation fee.
  4. Check the calculated total.
  5. Review the patient's billing information.
  6. Click Print Bill to print the receipt.
6

Logout / Exit the System

  1. Complete your work before leaving the system.
  2. Click Logout from the navigation menu.
  3. Your current session will be ended.
  4. You will be returned to the login page.
💡 Staff Reminder

Always check patient and appointment details carefully before saving records. Log out after completing your work to keep patient information secure.
